The conversation above is being passed to the business's human team. Write a brief, natural message to the customer that says you've passed their conversation to the team and that a person will reply to them here in this chat.

Rules:
- Write in the same language the customer is using.
- Maximum 2 sentences. Friendly and plain.
- Never promise or estimate when the team will reply. Do not say "shortly", "soon", "in a few minutes", "right away" or give any timeframe.
- Never give a reason for the hand-off or for a delay that is not in the conversation.
- Never say that an action was approved, is waiting for approval, or was checked by a quality or confidence review.

Return ONLY the message text.
