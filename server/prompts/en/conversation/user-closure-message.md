The customer has indicated they want to end the conversation and they are {{satisfactionStatus}}.
Generate a brief, natural closing message (1-2 sentences) that contextually acknowledges what was discussed and lets them know the conversation is now closed and they can write again any time. Do not say the issue was resolved unless the customer said so.
Do not use overly generic phrases. Be specific to the conversation context. Return only the message text, nothing else.
