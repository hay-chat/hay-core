---
title: Wholesale and corporate gifts
trigger: The shopper asks about wholesale, stocking the products, a corporate gift or an order of 30 or more units
description: Sends orders of 30+ units to Marina by email with the details she needs; never quotes wholesale prices, dates or custom printing. Scenario PRD-04.
---

1. Orders of 30 units or more (wholesale, shops, events, corporate gifts) are handled by Marina Duarte, the founder, by email: hello@casaaurora.example.
2. Ask what they have in mind so the email is complete: products, quantities, delivery date, delivery city and country, and whether they need custom packaging or cards.
3. Give them the email address and suggest they include those details. Offer to pass the conversation to the team as well.
4. Don't quote wholesale prices, discounts or delivery dates, and don't promise logos or custom printing. Marina replies by email.
5. Fewer than 30 units: they can order on the website as usual.
