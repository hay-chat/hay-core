# Casa Aurora: the demo company

A fake home-goods brand used to test Hay.chat end to end. Everything here is invented: no real
customer, address, phone number or card appears anywhere in this folder. Shopper emails use
`example.com`, so nothing can reach a real inbox.

## The business

- **What it sells:** hand-poured soy candles, ceramic plant pots and vases, stoneware cups and mugs,
  linen and cotton napkins, table linen, small serving pieces. Products are made in small batches, so
  colour and glaze vary slightly from piece to piece. Candles and ceramics break in transit more often
  than other goods.
- **Founded:** 2021 in São Paulo by Marina Duarte. Sells in three markets, each on a different platform:

| Store                   | Platform    | Market            | Currency | Languages      | Payments                          | Carriers            |
| ----------------------- | ----------- | ----------------- | -------- | -------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Casa Aurora (US)        | Shopify     | United States     | USD      | en             | card (Shopify Payments), PayPal   | USPS, UPS           |
| Casa Aurora Europe      | WooCommerce | Portugal + EU     | EUR      | en, pt-PT      | card, bank transfer (BACS), MB WAY | CTT, DPD           |
| Casa Aurora Brasil      | Nuvemshop   | Brazil            | BRL      | pt-BR          | Pix, boleto, card (Mercado Pago)  | Correios (PAC, SEDEX), Jadlog |

The Brazilian store also sells on Mercado Livre and Shopee. Those orders are handled in the
marketplace apps, not in Nuvemshop.

## Policies (the knowledge base states these; playbooks follow them)

- **Shipping.**
  - US: free over $75, otherwise $6.90. Ships within 2 business days; 3–7 business days in transit.
  - Europe: free in Portugal over €50 (€3.90 below), €7.90 to the rest of the EU. Ships within 2 business days;
    2–4 days in Portugal, 4–8 days in the EU.
  - Brasil: free over R$299. PAC 5–12 business days, SEDEX 1–4 business days. Ships within 2 business days.
- **Returns.** 30 days from delivery, unused and in the original packaging. Burned candles can't be
  returned (hygiene and safety) unless they're faulty. In Brazil, online orders also have the 7-day
  right of withdrawal from delivery (CDC Art. 49); the AI states the deadline and hands off, never
  computes refunds itself.
- **Damaged in transit.** Report within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the box. Casa Aurora
  sends a replacement or a refund, the shopper's choice. No need to send the broken item back.
- **Refunds.** Back to the original payment method. Card: 5–10 business days to show. Pix: same day once
  approved. Boleto: bank transfer to an account the shopper gives, 3–5 business days.
- **Cancellations.** Possible until the order ships. After shipping, the shopper returns it.
- **Order changes.** Address or scent/colour swaps only before shipping.
- **Goodwill.** Support may offer a 10 % apology coupon, one per shopper. Anything larger needs Marina.
- **Wholesale and corporate gifts:** orders of 30+ units go to Marina by email (hello@casaaurora.example).
- **Candle safety:** trim the wick to 5 mm, burn 2–4 hours at a time, never leave unattended.
- **Ceramic care:** pots have a drainage hole and are frost-safe to −5 °C; mugs are dishwasher and
  microwave safe except the gold-rim line.
