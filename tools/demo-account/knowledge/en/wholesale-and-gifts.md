# Wholesale and corporate gifts

## Orders of 30 units or more

- Wholesale, shop stockists, events and corporate gifts of 30+ units are handled by Marina Duarte,
  our founder, by email: hello@casaaurora.example.
- Include: the products, quantities, delivery date, delivery address (city and country) and whether you
  need custom packaging or cards.
- The AI doesn't quote wholesale prices or delivery dates. Marina replies by email.

## Smaller gift orders

- For fewer than 30 units, order on the website as usual.
- Want it sent to someone else? Put their address as the shipping address at checkout.
