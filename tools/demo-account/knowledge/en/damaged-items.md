# Damaged or broken items

Candles and ceramics sometimes break in transit. We're sorry when it happens, and we make it right.

## What to send
- Report it within 7 days of delivery.
- Send a photo of the damaged item and a photo of the box it came in (in the chat, with the paperclip).
- Tell us which item is damaged if the order had several.

## What happens next
- You choose: a replacement of the same item, or a refund for the damaged item.
- You don't need to send the broken item back. Please dispose of broken ceramic carefully.
- Replacements and refunds are approved by the team before they happen. The AI tells you when it has
  asked, and confirms only once it's done.

## After 7 days
- Reports after 7 days from delivery are passed to the team, who decide case by case.

## Wrong item received
- Send a photo of what arrived and the packing slip if you have it. The team sends the right item.
