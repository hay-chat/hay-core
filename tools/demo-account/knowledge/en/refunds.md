# Refunds and refund timelines

Refunds always go back to the original payment method.

## When the refund is made

- Cancelled before shipping: the team refunds once the cancellation is done.
- Returned item: once the return arrives and is checked.
- Damaged in transit: once the team approves the refund (no need to send the item back).

## How long until you see the money

- Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Shopify Payments): 5–10 business days to show on your statement after
  the refund is made. The exact day depends on your bank.
- PayPal (US store): back to your PayPal account.
- MB WAY (Europe store): back to the MB WAY account used to pay.
- Bank transfer (Europe store): the team sends the money back by bank transfer, by hand. They confirm
  the account with you first.

## Checking a refund

- The AI can look up whether a refund was made and on what date. It quotes the date from the order and
  never promises a refund that the order doesn't show.
- If 10 business days have passed since a card refund and it still isn't on your statement, the team
  can send you the refund details to show your bank.

## Partial refunds

- If only part of an order was refunded (for example one broken mug), the refund is for that item only.
  The AI explains which items were refunded and the amount.
