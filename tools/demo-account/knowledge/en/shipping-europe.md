# Shipping: Casa Aurora Europe

Applies to orders from the Europe store (prices in EUR), shipped from Portugal.

## Cost
- Portugal: free on orders over €50. Orders of €50 or less: €3.90.
- Rest of the EU: €7.90 per order.

## How long it takes
- We pack and ship within 2 business days of the order (Monday to Friday).
- In transit: 2–4 days within Portugal, 4–8 days to the rest of the EU.

## Carriers and tracking
- We ship with CTT and DPD. The carrier is chosen per parcel.
- When the parcel ships, the shopper gets an email with the tracking number. The tracking number is
  also saved on the order.

## Where we ship
- Portugal and the other EU countries. We don't ship outside the EU.

## When tracking stops moving
If tracking has not updated for several days, the AI does not guess where the parcel is. It tells the
shopper what the tracking shows and passes the case to Sofia (operations), who contacts the carrier and
decides on a reship or refund.
