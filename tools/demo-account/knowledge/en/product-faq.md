# Product FAQ

For prices and stock, the AI checks the store's live catalog. This page covers what the products are
made of and how big they are. If a question isn't answered here or in the catalog, the AI says it
doesn't know and offers to pass it to the team.

## Candles

- **Wax:** 100 % soy wax, paraffin-free, hand-poured in small batches in São Paulo, Brazil.
- **Wicks:** cotton, free of lead and zinc.
- **Allergens:** the wax and wicks of the Aurora candle and the gift set are allergen-free. The
  Lavender Travel Tin fragrance contains linalool, a natural part of lavender oil (listed on the label).
- **Aurora Soy Candle:** Sandalwood, Fig, Orange Blossom or Eucalyptus. Amber glass jar 8 cm wide ×
  9.5 cm tall, 220 g of wax, about 45 hours of burn time.
- **Lavender Travel Tin:** lavender with a note of cedar. Tin with a screw lid, 7 × 5 cm, 100 g of wax,
  about 20 hours.
- **Candle Trio Gift Set:** three 80 g minis (Sandalwood, Fig, Orange Blossom), about 18 hours each, in
  a recycled kraft box with a blank gift card.
- **Terra Three-Wick Candle:** amber and tonka bean, 600 g of wax in a glazed stoneware bowl 14 × 7 cm,
  about 60 hours.
- **Brass Wick Trimmer:** solid brass, 18 cm, reaches into jars up to 10 cm deep. Not dishwasher safe.
- Care and safety: see "Candle care and safety".

## Ceramics and glass

- **Glaze:** every glaze is lead-free.
- **Stoneware Mug:** 350 ml, 9 cm tall × 8.5 cm wide (12 cm with the handle), in Sand, Slate or Sage.
  Also sold as a set of 4 (Sand, Slate, Sage and Clay).
- **Lua Stoneware Tumbler:** 250 ml, no handle, in Sand or Slate.
- **Gold-Rim Espresso Cups (set of 2):** porcelain, 90 ml cups with saucers. Hand wash only, not
  microwave safe.
- **Terracotta Plant Pot:** 14 × 13 cm, fits a 12 cm nursery pot, saucer included. Terracotta or
  Whitewash.
- **Speckled Stoneware Planter:** Medium 22 × 20 cm (3.4 kg) or Large 30 × 27 cm (5.2 kg). No saucer.
- **Mini Succulent Pots (set of 3):** 7, 8 and 9 cm wide, in sage, sand and clay. They nest.
- **Arco Bud Vase:** 16 cm tall, 2.5 cm opening, watertight. White or Sand.
- **Mar Glass Vase:** mouth-blown recycled glass, 24 cm tall, about 1.4 litres. Sea Blue or Clear.
- **Large Stoneware Serving Bowl:** 28 cm wide, 2.8 litres, oven safe to 200 °C.
- **Stoneware Pitcher:** 1 litre, sand glaze.
- Care: see "Ceramic, pot and mug care".

## Linen, cotton and wood

- **Linen Napkins (set of 4):** stonewashed European flax linen, 45 × 45 cm. Natural, Terracotta, Sage
  or Charcoal. Machine wash at 40 °C, tumble dry low, no bleach. May shrink up to 3 % on the first wash.
- **Gingham Cotton Napkins (set of 6):** organic cotton, 40 × 40 cm, 3 terracotta and 3 sage. Machine
  wash up to 60 °C.
- **Linen Tablecloth:** 150 × 150 cm (seats 4) or 150 × 250 cm (seats 6–8). Undyed. Wash at 40 °C,
  may shrink up to 3 %.
- **Linen Table Runner:** 40 × 200 cm plus fringe, Natural or Charcoal.
- **Oak Serving Tray:** solid FSC-certified oak, 45 × 30 cm. Hand wash with a damp cloth; oil monthly
  with food-safe mineral oil. Made in Portugal.
- **Olive Wood Salad Servers:** 30 cm, from pruning offcuts. Hand wash; oil occasionally.
