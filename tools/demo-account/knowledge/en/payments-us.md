# Payments: Casa Aurora (US)

## Ways to pay
- Credit and debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) through Shopify Payments.
- PayPal.
- Prices are in USD and include no hidden fees. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.

## Payment pending
- An order shows "payment pending" when the payment hasn't gone through yet.
- The AI can email the payment link for an unpaid order to the email on the order. It can't send it to
  a different address, and it never takes card details in the chat.
- Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically after a while. A new order can be placed any time.

## Never share card details in the chat
We never ask for a card number, CVV or password. Photos of cards are hidden from the AI.
