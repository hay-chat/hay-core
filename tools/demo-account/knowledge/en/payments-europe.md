# Payments: Casa Aurora Europe

## Ways to pay

- Credit and debit card.
- MB WAY (Portugal).
- Bank transfer (BACS).
- Prices are in EUR and include VAT.

## Paying by bank transfer

- After checkout, the order waits "on hold" until the transfer arrives.
- The bank details and the reference to use are on the order confirmation email and the order page.
  Always put the order number as the transfer reference.
- Transfers can take 1–2 business days to arrive. We ship once the money is in.
- If the confirmation email didn't arrive, the AI can ask the store to resend it to the email on the
  order. It can't send it to a different address.

## Unpaid orders

- Orders that are never paid are cancelled automatically. A new order can be placed any time.

## Never share card details in the chat

We never ask for a card number, CVV or password. Photos of cards are hidden from the AI.
