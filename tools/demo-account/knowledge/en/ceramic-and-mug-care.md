# Ceramic, pot and mug care

Each piece is made in small batches, so colour and glaze vary slightly from piece to piece. That's part
of the handmade character, not a defect.

## Plant pots and vases
- Pots have a drainage hole. Use a saucer indoors.
- Frost-safe down to −5 °C. Bring them inside in harder frost.

## Cups and mugs
- Dishwasher safe and microwave safe.
- **Exception: the gold-rim line.** Gold-rim pieces are hand wash only and must never go in the
  microwave (the metal rim can spark and the gold wears off in the dishwasher).

## Everyday care
- Avoid sudden temperature changes, for example pouring boiling water into a mug straight from the
  fridge.
- Small surface marks from the glaze are normal. Chips or cracks on arrival are damage: send a photo in
  the chat within 7 days of delivery.
