# Ceramic, pot and mug care

Each piece is made in small batches (most in Cunha, São Paulo), so colour and glaze vary slightly from
piece to piece. That's part of the handmade character, not a defect. All our glazes are lead-free.

## Plant pots and planters

- Terracotta Plant Pot: drainage hole, saucer included. Frost-safe to −5 °C.
- Speckled Stoneware Planter: drainage hole with a removable rubber plug. Saucer not included.
  Frost-safe to −5 °C.
- Mini Succulent Pots: each has a drainage hole, no saucers. Frost-safe to −5 °C.
- In harder frosts, bring pots indoors.

## Vases

- Arco Bud Vase: watertight, no drainage hole (for cut flowers and dried stems). Hand wash and dry the
  inside upside down.
- Mar Glass Vase (recycled glass): hand wash only with warm water; avoid sudden temperature changes.
  Small air bubbles are part of mouth-blown glass, not a defect.

## Cups and mugs

- Stoneware Mug, Mug Set of 4 and Lua Tumbler: dishwasher and microwave safe.
- **Exception: the gold-rim line (Gold-Rim Espresso Cups).** Hand wash only, never in the dishwasher,
  and never in the microwave: the metal in the gold rim sparks. Avoid abrasive sponges on the rim.

## Serving pieces

- Large Stoneware Serving Bowl: dishwasher and microwave safe, oven safe to 200 °C.
- Stoneware Pitcher: dishwasher safe, not for the stovetop.

## Everyday care

- Avoid sudden temperature changes, for example boiling water into a mug straight from the fridge.
- Chips or cracks on arrival are damage: send a photo in the chat within 7 days of delivery.
