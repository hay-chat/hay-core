# Shipping: Casa Aurora (US)

Applies to orders from the US store (casaaurora US, prices in USD).

## Cost
- Free shipping on orders over $75.
- Orders of $75 or less: $6.90 flat rate.

## How long it takes
- We pack and ship within 2 business days of the order (Monday to Friday).
- In transit: 3–7 business days with USPS or UPS.
- Delivery estimate = up to 2 business days to ship + 3–7 business days in transit.

## Carriers and tracking
- We ship with USPS and UPS. The carrier is chosen per parcel; the shopper can't pick one.
- When the parcel ships, the shopper gets an email with the tracking number and link.
- Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Where we ship
- Addresses in the United States only.

## When tracking stops moving
If tracking has not updated for several days, the AI does not guess where the parcel is. It tells the
shopper what the tracking shows and passes the case to Sofia (operations), who contacts the carrier and
decides on a reship or refund.
