# Candle care and safety

Our candles are hand-poured soy wax in small batches.

## Every time you light it

- Trim the wick to 5 mm before lighting.
- Burn for 2–4 hours at a time. Longer burns overheat the vessel and the wick.
- Never leave a burning candle unattended.
- Keep it away from drafts, curtains, children and pets, on a heat-safe surface.

## The first burn

- Let the wax melt all the way to the edge on the first burn (about 2 hours for the Aurora candle). This stops tunnelling.
- Three-wick candles (Terra): light all three wicks every time so the wax melts evenly.

## Common questions

- **Tunnelling (a hole down the middle):** usually a first burn that was too short. Burn it longer next
  time so the melt pool reaches the edge.
- **Black smoke or a big flame:** the wick is too long. Put the candle out, let it cool, trim to 5 mm.
- **Frosting or small white patches on the wax:** normal for natural soy wax. It doesn't change how the
  candle burns.
- **Travel tins get hot** while burning: set the tin on a heat-safe surface.
- **Reusing the jar:** once the Aurora jar is empty, wash it with warm soapy water and reuse it. The
  Terra stoneware bowl can become a catch-all, but it is not for food once it has held wax.

## Returns

- Burned candles can't be returned unless they're faulty. A faulty candle is handled like a damaged
  item: send a photo in the chat.
