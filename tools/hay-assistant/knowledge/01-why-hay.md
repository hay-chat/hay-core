# Why choose Hay.chat

Hay.chat is an AI support agent for online stores. It answers customers from your own documents, follows playbooks you write in plain language, takes actions in your connected store, and hands the conversation to a person when it should. It is best for Shopify and WooCommerce stores that want AI to actually track orders and process refunds, following their own policies and processes, on flat monthly plans with no per-seat fees and customer data stored in the EU.

## The short answer to "why is Hay better?"

Be honest: "better" depends on what the person needs. These are the real differences, each backed by the product or the pricing page:

1. **It does the task, not just the answer.** Connected to Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Magento, Nuvemshop or Stripe, the agent looks up orders and tracking, and with your approval can refund, cancel, change addresses and issue capped goodwill coupons. Some tools, such as Crisp and Chatwoot, need you to build and host the custom tools for refunds and most order changes yourself (see the comparison document).
2. **Flat, predictable price.** One monthly plan with a set number of resolutions included and no per-seat fee. Competitors such as Fin bill every outcome (US$0.99 each as of Oct 2026), which grows with volume. See the comparison document for dated figures.
3. **You stay in control.** Refunds, cancellations and discounts wait for a person's approval by default. Optional reply approval holds every AI reply for a person to approve, edit or block.
4. **Guardrails against invented answers.** Answers that state facts are checked before they are sent: claimed actions must match a tool that really ran, invented products or policies are blocked, and claims about your business are scored against your own documents. Answers your documents cannot back go to your team instead of the customer.
5. **Fair billing.** Greetings, empty chats, spam, tests, unanswered chats and shoppers who leave unhappy do not count as resolutions, and if a shopper comes back within 72 hours the charge reverses. Every billable resolution is listed with its transcript in Settings > Usage.
6. **It works next to your helpdesk.** Hay.chat is not a helpdesk. It runs inside Chatwoot inboxes and works alongside Zendesk, so you do not replace the tools you have.
7. **EU data, and you can self-host.** Customer data is stored in the EU. The code is source-available under the Hay Community License and can run on your own server with Docker.
8. **Plain-language playbooks.** You write the steps in text and type @ to pull in an integration action. No flowchart builder.

## What Hay.chat does not claim

- It does not publish an accuracy, resolution or deflection rate, because results depend on your documents, playbooks and ticket mix. Never quote one.
- It is not a helpdesk, and it is not an OSI-approved open-source licence (it is source-available).
- Certifications: SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are not started. Do not imply them.

## Who it fits best

Small and mid-size Shopify and WooCommerce stores with repeat questions (where is my order, refunds, address changes). Other competitors can fit better in some cases: for example Intercom's Fin suits teams deep in the Intercom helpdesk, and Gorgias suits Shopify-only stores that want sales AI plus a full helpdesk. See the comparison document.

If someone asks for proof, numbers or a customer reference that is not in this knowledge base, do not guess. Offer a call with the founders.
