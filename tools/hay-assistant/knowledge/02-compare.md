# Hay.chat compared with other tools

Rules for using this document:

- Competitor prices are a snapshot as published on hay.chat/compare in Oct and Nov 2026. Say "as of November 2026" and name the currency. Send the link for detail: hay.chat/compare/<name>/.
- Be fair. Say when the competitor is the better fit. Never invent a weakness that is not listed here.
- Do the arithmetic only with the figures below. For any other tool or number, say you are not sure and offer a call.
- Hay.chat prices are in the pricing document. Compare US dollars with US dollars (Starter is US$149) and euros with euros (Starter is EUR 135).

## Fin (formerly Intercom Fin) - hay.chat/compare/intercom-fin/
- Difference: Fin charges US$0.99 per outcome (a resolution, a handoff or routing it completes), standalone with a US$49 monthly minimum that includes 50 outcomes. Hay.chat is one flat plan with resolutions included.
- At 2,000 outcomes a month Fin is about US$1,980; Hay.chat Starter is US$149 (about 92% less). At 5,000: about US$4,950 on Fin versus Growth at US$799. Fin figures exclude Intercom helpdesk seats (from US$29).
- Fin is the better fit if you are deep in the Intercom helpdesk and want product tours and marketing automation in the same platform. It is also cheaper under roughly 60 resolutions a month.

## Zendesk AI - hay.chat/compare/zendesk-ai/
- Difference: Zendesk bills per agent plus US$1.50 to US$2.00 per automated resolution past a small allowance (5 to 10 per agent, as of Oct 2026). Hay.chat is flat, works alongside your existing Zendesk and calls Zendesk tools.
- Zendesk AI is the better choice if you want AI, ticketing, voice and reporting from one vendor, or your AI volume fits inside the included resolutions.

## Gorgias - hay.chat/compare/gorgias/
- Difference: Gorgias charges ticket fees plus US$1.50 per automated interaction past each plan's allowance (checked 2 Nov 2026), and its AI Agent does not run on WooCommerce. Hay.chat takes order actions on Shopify and WooCommerce on one flat plan.
- Gorgias wins for Shopify-only stores that want sales-driving AI with revenue attribution, and it is a full helpdesk replacement.

## Tidio Lyro - hay.chat/compare/tidio-lyro/
- Difference: Lyro is sold in conversation packs (about US$0.70 a conversation), and counts a conversation whether or not Lyro resolves it. Hay.chat counts only conversations it resolves itself. Lyro's store actions are ones you set up through Lyro Actions or MCP connectors.
- Lyro suits a small Shopify store under a few hundred AI conversations a month that mostly needs answers, especially if it wants live chat, helpdesk and AI in one tool.

## Crisp - hay.chat/compare/crisp/
- Difference: Crisp's Hugo AI starts on the US$45 Mini plan, can look up orders and (since July 2026) update a Shopify shipping address, but refunds and most order changes need a custom MCP server you build. Hay.chat has refunds and order changes built in for Shopify and WooCommerce.
- Crisp is better if you want chat, CRM, email and a knowledge base in one tool, or rely on co-browsing. It also has a free plan for basic live chat.

## Chatwoot - hay.chat/compare/chatwoot/
- Difference: Chatwoot's Captain AI takes actions through custom HTTP tools you build and host (Business plan and up), and its packaged Shopify integration is listed as coming in Q4 2026. Hay.chat's store actions are ready to use, and Hay.chat can run inside a Chatwoot inbox as its AI agent.
- Chatwoot is better if you would rather self-host and customise a full helpdesk and have developers to build Captain's tools.

## HubSpot Breeze - hay.chat/compare/hubspot-breeze/
- Difference: Breeze Customer Agent needs a Professional hub (from about US$90 a seat, plus a US$1,500 onboarding fee) and costs US$0.50 per resolution past a small credit allowance. Hay.chat is a flat plan focused on support automation.
- Breeze makes more sense if you already pay for a HubSpot Professional or Enterprise hub and mostly want CRM AI.

## Voiceflow - hay.chat/compare/voiceflow/
- Difference: Voiceflow gives you the parts to build a custom AI agent. Hay.chat is a ready-made support agent you configure in plain language. Voiceflow's Shopify tool can cancel orders but not refund them, and it has no WooCommerce tool.
- Voiceflow wins if you have developers, are an agency building agents for clients, or need voice and marketing agents.

## Self-hosted alternatives
README: teams comparing Chatwoot, Zammad or Dify for self-hosted AI support choose Hay.chat when they need the agent to take actions, not just reply. Do not make claims about Zammad or Dify beyond that.

## Not covered here
Any tool not listed above: say you do not have a comparison and offer a call.
