# Privacy, GDPR and EU data

Hay.chat is operated by Hay Labs LDA, based in Lisbon, Portugal (Portuguese law). Contact: hello@hay.chat; privacy requests: privacy@hay.chat.

## What you can say with confidence
- **Customer data is stored in the EU** and encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Backups are encrypted and taken daily.
- **AI processing may happen outside the EU.** AI replies are generated by AI providers such as OpenAI (US), with transfers covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses; OpenAI's API terms say it does not train on API data. Hay.chat does not currently guarantee EU-only AI processing. An EU AI endpoint is planned but is not live. Never say "all data stays in the EU" or "EU-only AI". If a prospect needs EU-only AI processing, say so plainly and offer a call (or the self-hosted option, where you choose your own provider).
- **A Data Processing Agreement** is part of every customer agreement: hay.chat/data-processing-agreement/. Sub-processors are listed in Annex C (hay.chat/annex-c-subprocessors/); processing details in Annex A and security measures (Art. 32 GDPR) in Annex B.
- **Separation between customers:** each workspace's data is isolated by organisation ID, enforced in every database query. Roles in a workspace: owner, admin, member, viewer (plus agent and contributor roles in the user list). API keys carry only the permissions you give them.
- **Retention:** conversations stay until you delete them or until a retention period you set in workspace settings runs out (none by default). Closed conversations older than the period are anonymized: messages and search embeddings are deleted, non-personal details like timestamps are kept. Conversations can be put on legal hold.
- **Customer data requests (GDPR DSAR):** Hay.chat supports data export and deletion requests for end customers, with email verification. In the dashboard see Settings > Privacy and Settings > Customer Privacy.
- **AI disclosure:** AI replies carry an AI label; EU workspaces cannot hide it.
- **Breach notification:** the DPA commits to telling customers without undue delay; the internal target is within 72 hours of confirming a breach.
- **When a plan ends:** the AI stops answering and the dashboard locks; data is kept for 90 days and can be downloaded by the owner or an admin in that time, then deleted.
- **Telemetry:** the dashboard product analytics mask text and inputs. Self-hosted installs send no telemetry unless configured.
- Service status: status.hay.chat. Report a vulnerability to hello@hay.chat with reproduction steps.

## Certifications
SOC 2 and ISO 27001: not started. DigitalOcean's data centres hold ISO 27001/27017/27018, but that is the hosting provider's certification, not Hay.chat's. Never claim Hay.chat is certified or "GDPR certified". Say it is built for GDPR compliance with a DPA.

## Do not answer, offer a call
Legal advice, whether Hay.chat is suitable for a specific regulated industry, health or payment-card data handling, custom DPAs, security questionnaires, penetration test reports, and exact data-centre locations.
