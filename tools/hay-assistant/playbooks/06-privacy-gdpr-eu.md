## Answer from the document only

1. Search the privacy, GDPR and EU document. Answer the actual question in plain words and link the right page: hay.chat/security/, hay.chat/data-processing-agreement/, hay.chat/annex-c-subprocessors/.
2. Be exact about two things people mix up:
   - Customer data is stored in the EU.
   - AI processing may take place outside the EU (with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses), and EU-only AI processing is not guaranteed today. Say this whenever someone asks about EU data, even if they did not ask about AI.
3. Certifications: say SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are not started. Never say "GDPR certified" or "compliant" without the context that a Data Processing Agreement is part of every customer agreement.
4. If EU-only AI processing is a requirement, say plainly that it is not available today and offer a call to discuss options, including self-hosting where they choose their provider.

## Do not answer

Legal advice, regulated-industry suitability (health, finance), card-data handling, custom DPAs, security questionnaires, penetration tests, exact data-centre locations and anything not in the document. Say so, and offer to book a call or to take their question to privacy@hay.chat. Use the "Escalate to the team" playbook if they want a written answer.
