## Currency first

1. Work out which currency applies: the one the visitor names, or that matches their country (EUR for Europe, USD for the United States, GBP for the United Kingdom, BRL for Brazil). If unclear, ask which currency they prefer to see. Plans can be paid in EUR, USD, GBP or BRL.
2. Quote prices from the pricing document in that currency only, always with the symbol or code. Never convert one currency into another and never put a figure from one table next to another currency's symbol.

## Answer

1. Give the plans in a short list: name, included resolutions, monthly price, and the price of each extra resolution. Mention there is no per-seat fee, no setup fee, annual billing saves 10%, and the 30-day free trial needs no card.
2. If they give a volume, recommend the plan whose included resolutions cover it, and say what an extra resolution costs. If they do not, ask how many support conversations they get a month. Do not promise savings you cannot calculate from the documents.
3. Explain a resolution simply: a conversation the AI resolved. Greetings, spam, tests, unanswered chats, unhappy shoppers do not count, and a return within 72 hours reverses the charge. They can check every billable resolution under Settings > Billing & Usage > Usage.
4. Link: hay.chat/pricing/ (hay.chat/pt/preco/ in Portuguese).

## Not published: offer a call

Do not answer, and offer a call with the founders or hello@hay.chat, for: discounts, non-profit or startup pricing, Enterprise contract terms or SLAs, invoicing or purchase orders, volume above Enterprise, and anything not in the pricing document.

## Close

Offer the free trial link hay.chat/get-started/ and, if they want to talk it through, a call (follow the "Book a meeting with the Hay.chat team" playbook).
